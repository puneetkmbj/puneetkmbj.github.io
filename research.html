Research

My research examines the strategic, technical, and market dimensions of energy transition in emerging and oil-producing economies. Three themes organize most of the work: (i) decarbonization pathways and scenario analysis, typically using integrated assessment models; (ii) the ecosystem conditions — policy, regulation, market design — needed for emerging clean energy technologies such as hydrogen and CCUS to move from strategy to deployment; and (iii) the political economy of transition in coal-dependent and hydrocarbon-exporting contexts.
